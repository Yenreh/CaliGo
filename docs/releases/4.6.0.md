Find addresses and places when planning a trip.

## What's new

- **Addresses and places** in the trip planner: where you choose where you leave from or where you are going, type an address such as "Cl. 4 #75-71" or a place such as "Unicentro", then tap **Search "…" as an address or place** or the keyboard's search key. The phone finds it by itself, with no account or key, on phones with Google Play services.
- When the exact number isn't known, you get the crossing of its two streets, or the MIO stop at that crossing, marked as approximate. Crossings can be typed too: "Calle 5 con Carrera 38".

## Installation

Download the **arm64-v8a** APK (or **armeabi-v7a** on old 32-bit phones) and install it over the previous version; your data stays. From the app: Settings > About > **Check for updates**.
