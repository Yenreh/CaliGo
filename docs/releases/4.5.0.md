Plan trips across Cali on the MIO, timed with the buses on their way.

## What's new

- **Plan a trip**: a new button in the bottom bar, and **Go here** on any stop. Choose where you leave from and where you are going (your location, a point on the map, a favorite, a station or any stop) and see up to five ways to get there, with the walks, the lines and where to change.
- **Live times**: each trip is timed with the buses actually on their way to each stop, including the changes. Anything marked **~** is approximate, with no bus yet to confirm it, and uses how often that line usually comes.
- **Lines sharing a stretch** show together; tap one to see its own route and platforms on the map.
- Open a trip to see it on the map and step by step, or compare it in **Google Maps**.
- **Sharp map** in Settings: turn it off to use less mobile data.

## Changed

- The bottom bar shows icons only: cards, search, plan a trip and favorites. Press and hold one to see its name.
- Arrivals and a line's buses refresh only while their screen is in view.
- Stops are placed on the map from the routes, with fewer requests to MIO.
- Lines missing from MIO's line list but seen running are used too.
- When MIO's route service fails, the last copy keeps working.

## Installation

Download the **arm64-v8a** APK (or **armeabi-v7a** on old 32-bit phones) and install it over the previous version; your data stays. From the app: Settings > About > **Check for updates**.
