A small change on top of 4.0.1.

## Changed

- Balances refresh on their own only when the app launches, not every time you come back to it from the background. The option is still in Settings > My cards.

## Installation

Download the **arm64-v8a** APK (or **armeabi-v7a** on old 32-bit phones) and install it over the previous version; your data stays. From the app: Settings > About > **Check for updates**.
