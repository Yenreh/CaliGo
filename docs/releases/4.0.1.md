A small fix on top of 4.0.0.

## Fixed

- The home screen buttons no longer cut their labels with a large system font: "My cards" is now just "Cards", and the labels shrink to fit when needed.

## Installation

Download the **arm64-v8a** APK (or **armeabi-v7a** on old 32-bit phones) and install it over 4.0.0; your data stays. From the app: Settings > About > **Check for updates**.
