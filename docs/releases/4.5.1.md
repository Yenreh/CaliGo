Small fixes on top of 4.5.0.

## Fixed

- On phones with navigation buttons, **Use this point** on the map, and the last items of Settings, cards and stop lists, no longer sit under the buttons.
- An open trip in the planner now folds only from its summary or when another one opens, so tapping a line button no longer closes it by accident.

## Changed

- Walks are timed at a slower, more realistic pace, so trips and the buses you can still catch account for the time on foot better.

## Installation

Download the **arm64-v8a** APK (or **armeabi-v7a** on old 32-bit phones) and install it over the previous version; your data stays. From the app: Settings > About > **Check for updates**.
